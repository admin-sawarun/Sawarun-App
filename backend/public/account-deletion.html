Delete Your Savarun Account

How to permanently delete your Savarun account and data.

You can permanently delete your Savarun account (operated by SAWARUN TECH PRIVATE LIMITED) and all associated data at any time.

Option 1: Delete in the app (instant)

  1. Open the Savarun app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account.
  3. Confirm. Your account and data are deleted immediately.

Option 2: Request by email

If you can no longer access the app, email us from any address with:

  • Subject: Delete my Savarun account
  • The phone number or email address you used to sign in to Savarun.

Email admin-sawarun@savarun.in

We may ask you to confirm ownership of the account. Email requests are completed within 30 days, and we will confirm by reply when it is done.

Delete some of your data without deleting your account

You can remove specific data and keep using Savarun.

In the app

  • Wardrobe items: open Wardrobe, tap an item, then tap the delete icon. The item, its photo and its tags are deleted.
  • Saved outfit sets: open Wardrobe, tap the Outfit Sets button at the top, then tap the delete icon on an outfit.

By email

To delete anything else, such as specific posts, chat conversations, outfit photos and AI analysis history, or your profile photo and bio, email us from the email address or with the phone number you use to sign in:

  • Subject: Delete some of my Savarun data
  • Your phone number or email address used to sign in, and which data you want deleted.

Email admin-sawarun@savarun.in

We complete these requests within 30 days and confirm by reply. Deleted data cannot be recovered. The limits in "What may be kept" below also apply.

What is deleted

  • Your login account (Google, Apple or phone number sign-in)
  • Your profile: name, username, profile photo, bio and style preferences
  • Outfit photos and AI analysis history
  • Wardrobe items, their photos and tags, and saved outfit sets
  • Your posts and their images, your likes, and your followers / following connections
  • Chat conversations you are part of, including messages and images
  • Brand applications and products you submitted

What may be kept

  • Reports you filed against other users are kept for safety records, with your identity removed.
  • Affiliate click counts are kept as anonymous totals without your user ID.
  • Information we must keep by law (for example, records relating to illegal content reported to authorities) and short-lived server security logs.
Deletion is permanent. Once your account is deleted it cannot be recovered. You can create a new account later, but your previous content will not be restored.

Questions? Contact admin-sawarun@savarun.in. See also our Privacy Policy.
