Privacy Policy

How SAWARUN TECH PRIVATE LIMITED collects, uses and protects your data in the Savarun app.

Last updated: November 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SAWARUN TECH PRIVATE LIMITED ("Savarun", "we", "us"), a company in India, collects, uses, shares and protects information when you use the Savarun mobile app and related services (the "Service"). By using the Service you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

Account and login information

  • Sign-in identifiers: we use Google Firebase Authentication. Depending on the method you choose, we receive your email address and name (Sign in with Google or Sign in with Apple, including Apple's private relay email if you choose to hide your email) or your mobile phone number (phone number login with a one-time password). We also store a unique user ID.
  • Profile information you provide: display name, username, profile photo, bio and style preferences.

Content you create

  • Outfit photos you capture or upload for AI analysis, and the resulting analysis (Fit Score, style breakdown, detected clothing, colours and feedback) saved to your outfit history.
  • Wardrobe items: photos of clothing you add and the tags generated by AI or entered by you (category, colour, fabric, season, formality), wear counts and saved outfit sets.
  • Social activity: posts you publish (image, caption, style tag), likes, follows and followers, and chat messages and images you send to other users, including chat requests.
  • Brand applications: if you apply to list a brand, the brand name, logo, product details, images and website links you submit.
  • Reports you file about users, posts or messages, and the reason you give.

Usage information

  • Affiliate clicks: when you tap a partner product, we record the product, brand, your user ID and the time, to count clicks for brands.
  • Activity: the time you were last active (used to count daily active users) and standard server logs (IP address, request time and path, app user agent) kept for security and troubleshooting.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts or payment card details, and the app does not use advertising identifiers. The app does not currently use push notifications, so no push/device token is collected.

2. How we use information

  • To create and secure your account and let you sign in.
  • To analyse outfit and clothing photos with AI and show you scores, tags and style suggestions.
  • To run social features: your profile, posts, feed, likes, follows and chat.
  • To show partner brand products and measure affiliate clicks.
  • To keep the community safe: review reports, remove content and suspend or ban abusive accounts.
  • To operate, maintain, debug and improve the Service, and to comply with law.

3. How information is shared

We do not sell your personal data and we do not show third-party advertising. We share information only with:

  • Other users: your username, display name, profile photo, bio, posts, likes and follower counts are visible to other users. Chat messages are visible to the people in that conversation.
  • Service providers (processors) that act on our behalf:
    • Google Firebase (Google LLC): Authentication, Cloud Firestore database and Cloud Storage for photos.
    • AI vision provider: outfit and clothing photos you submit for analysis are sent to OpenAI's API to generate the analysis. Under OpenAI's API data policy, data sent through its API is not used to train its models.
    • Railway (Railway Corporation): hosting for our backend server.
  • Partner brands: when you tap a product, you are taken to the brand's own website. Any purchase or information you give there is governed by that brand's policies. We share only aggregate click counts with brands.
  • Legal reasons: when required by law, court order or a valid government request, or to protect the safety of users and the public (including reporting child sexual abuse material to the authorities).
  • Business transfers: in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, subject to this policy.

4. Data storage and security

Data is stored with Google Firebase and our hosting provider, whose servers may be located outside India (for example in the United States). Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Access to our systems is restricted, and requests to our server require a verified Firebase sign-in token. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

5. Retention

  • We keep your account data, photos and content for as long as your account is active.
  • When you delete content (for example a post or a wardrobe item), it is removed from our database.
  • When you delete your account, we delete your profile, outfit history, wardrobe, posts, likes, follows, chats, brand applications and uploaded photos (see section 6). Reports you filed are kept without your identity for moderation records, and affiliate click counts are kept without your user ID.
  • Server logs are kept for a limited period for security and then deleted. We may keep limited information longer if required by law or to resolve disputes or enforce our Terms.

6. Deleting your account

You can permanently delete your account and data at any time:

  • In the app: Profile → Settings → Delete Account.
  • On the web: follow the steps on our account deletion page, which includes an email option.

Deletion from the app takes effect immediately and cannot be undone.

7. Your rights and choices

Subject to applicable law (including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023), you may:

  • access and correct your profile information in the app;
  • delete individual content, or your whole account;
  • request a summary of the personal data we hold about you;
  • withdraw consent by deleting your account or stopping use of the Service;
  • raise a grievance with us and, if it is not resolved, with the Data Protection Board of India.

To exercise these rights, email admin-sawarun@savarun.in. We respond within 30 days.

8. Age requirement

Savarun is intended for users 18 and older; we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18, and we delete such accounts when we learn of them. If you believe a person under 18 is using Savarun, please contact us.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page with a new "Last updated" date and, for significant changes, notify you in the app.
