Child Safety Standards

Savarun's standards against child sexual abuse and exploitation.

Last updated: November 13, 2026

These Child Safety Standards describe how SAWARUN TECH PRIVATE LIMITED ("Savarun") prevents and responds to child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) on the Savarun app, which includes social features such as public posts, follows and private chat.

1. Zero tolerance

Savarun has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse material (CSAM) and any form of child sexual abuse or exploitation, including grooming, sextortion, sexualising minors, or using the Service to contact minors for sexual purposes. Such content and behaviour are strictly prohibited by our Terms & Conditions.

2. Age requirement

Savarun is intended for users 18 and older; we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18, and we delete such accounts when we learn of them.

3. In-app reporting

Any user can report a profile, post or chat message directly in the app using the Report option, and can block users. Reports can also be sent to admin-sawarun@savarun.in.

4. Review and enforcement

  • Reports are reviewed by our team, with reports involving minors prioritised, and in all cases within 24 hours.
  • Content that violates these standards is removed, and the accounts responsible are permanently banned.
  • We preserve relevant information as required by law to support investigations.

5. Cooperation with authorities

When we become aware of apparent CSAM or child exploitation, we report it to the relevant authorities, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and Indian authorities such as the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal (cybercrime.gov.in) and local law enforcement, as required under applicable law, including India's Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO) Act, 2012 and Information Technology Act, 2000. We cooperate fully with law enforcement requests.

6. Compliance

Savarun complies with applicable child safety laws and with the Google Play policy on child sexual abuse and exploitation.

7. Child safety contact

Our designated point of contact for child safety matters:
SAWARUN TECH PRIVATE LIMITED, India
Email: admin-sawarun@savarun.in

If a child is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services (112 in India) first.
